This paper is the result of a collaboration between the authors and AI systems.
Large language models (Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's GPT models) were used throughout: to survey
the literature, to propose constructions and proof strategies, to carry out and check
computations, to draft the text, and to check the arguments independently of one another.
Every result was checked repeatedly; the authors have read the final text and take full
responsibility for its content, including any remaining errors.

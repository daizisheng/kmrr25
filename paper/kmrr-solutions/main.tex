\begin{abstract}
We resolve a number of the problems collected by Kra, Moreira, Richter and Robertson in their survey
\emph{Problems on infinite sumset configurations in the integers and beyond}.
Our main result is that in every countably infinite abelian group, every set of positive upper Banach
density contains a sumset $B_1+\dots+B_k$ of $k$ infinite sets, for every $k$; the proof uses the
Kronecker factor, but no higher-order Host--Kra structure theory, nilsystems of step greater than one,
or nilspaces.
We also answer, positively or negatively, questions on monochromatic sets $B+mB$, on sumsets in
sets of logarithmic density, on polynomial sumset configurations, on sums of the form
$B_{i_1}+\dots+B_{i_m}$ with $B_i$ drawn from a density family, on random sets, on ordered sumsets in
shifted primes, on sums and products in $\N$ and $\Q$, and on sumsets in abelian groups. Several of
these answers concern the questions as literally stated, and two follow directly from known
examples; in each case we say so and discuss the natural corrected question.
Finally, answering a question of Kousek and Radi\'c, we show that the supremum of the lower Banach
densities of sets $A\subset\N$ such that $B+B+t\not\subset A$ for every infinite $B\subset\N$ and
every $t\ge0$ (respectively $B+B\not\subset A$ for every infinite $B$) is $1/2$ (respectively $3/4$).
The main theorem and most of the other results are formalized in Lean~4, with some published
theorems entering as explicit hypotheses.
\end{abstract}
